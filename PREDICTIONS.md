# Predictions

Before an experiment, write what you think will happen: one line, under the step's heading. Afterwards, add what actually happened, and mark it **hit** or **miss**. When it's a miss, add one line on why. That line is where the learning is.

Your tutor tallies your hits for three kinds of prediction, and you mark each one with its kind:
- **[code]:** about your code and its database;
- **[agent]:** about what an agent will do;
- **[harness]:** about how a change to your agent's setup changes what it does.
