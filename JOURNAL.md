# Journal

Your record of the course, one section per step, newest at the bottom. Your tutor reads it, and opens the next step once a step's section is here.

For each step, write:
- **Decisions:** for each "Decide first", your choice and why, written *before* you opened the answer. Then one line on whether you kept it, and why.
- **Experiments:** what the guide asks you to paste, like an agent exchange.
- **Review:** at the end of a milestone, each thing the reviewer found, and what you did about it: fixed, or why not.
- **Questions:** your tutor's questions for the step, and your answers, in your own words.

Write it for yourself in a month. Full sentences beat fragments.
